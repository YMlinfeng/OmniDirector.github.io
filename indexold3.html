Scene Generalization
[Module Overview] This section evaluates the model's zero-shot capability to generalize across diverse conceptual scenes. The system inherently adapts to drastic shifts in lighting, texturing, and geometry while enforcing rigorous camera motion constraints.
Multi Shot
[Module Overview] Maintaining visual coherence across completely different camera angles is a notorious challenge. This module demonstrates the ability to execute sequential jump cuts while perfectly retaining the storyline's logical continuity.
Dynamic Motion
[Module Overview] Dynamic motion breathes Hollywood-level vitality into generated visuals. The model seamlessly translates explicit trajectory constraints into breathtaking pans, tilts, and dolly shots without warping the fundamental geometry.
Special Camera Movement
[Module Overview] Traversing through complex spatial occlusion involves synthesizing parts of the environment that are initially invisible. This section pushes the boundary of 3D geometry hallucination through orbiting and fly-through motions.

Pipeline
An MLLM Director first interprets multimodal instructions into high-level semantics and tells a storyline. Guided by this global planning, the first stage generates a blueprint video. In the second stage, keyframes are extracted from the blueprint and used as first–last frame conditions for parallel sub-clip generation, refining local details and dynamics to synthesize long-duration videos.
