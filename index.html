Scene Generalization
[Module Overview] This section evaluates the model's zero-shot capability to generalize across diverse conceptual scenes. The system inherently adapts to drastic shifts in lighting, texturing, and geometry while enforcing rigorous camera motion constraints.
[Depth of Field Consistency] Notice how the focal length realistically simulates physical optics during the motion. The model actively tracks high-frequency textures in the background, preventing artifacts or texture swimming even under extreme spatial displacement. This exhibits a profound understanding of 3D contextual environments.
[Dynamic Lighting Adaptation] As the camera smoothly glides around the primary subject, the global illumination and specular reflections seamlessly recalculate in real-time. By leveraging spatial-temporal attention, the generative process maintains rigorous geometric volume without breaking the cinematic mood intended by the director's prompt.
[Subject Identity Preservation] Generating large-scale movement often distorts human or intricate object anatomy. Here, OmniDirector successfully isolates camera trajectory parameters from appearance features. The smooth dolly-out maneuver highlights unprecedented stability, preserving identity consistency down to the finest micro-details.
Multi Shot
[Module Overview] Maintaining visual coherence across completely different camera angles is a notorious challenge. This module demonstrates the ability to execute sequential jump cuts while perfectly retaining the storyline's logical continuity.
[Angle Transitioning] A textbook execution of a hard cut transition. The network seamlessly links a medium wide shot to a tight close-up, verifying that the multi-shot director module properly anchors latent object representations between entirely disjointed temporal windows.
[Narrative Framing] By incorporating a cinematic blueprint mechanism, the model anticipates the future frame's composition. Observe how the tracking shot smoothly establishes the spatial geometry before cutting to an alternate viewpoint without breaking the viewer's spatial awareness.
[Complex Continuity] This shot exemplifies high-level semantic alignment. Even as the camera switches across the 180-degree axis, lighting sources and character momentum are perfectly inherited. This proves the architecture's scalability when tasked with long-form video synthesis.
Dynamic Motion
[Module Overview] Dynamic motion breathes Hollywood-level vitality into generated visuals. The model seamlessly translates explicit trajectory constraints into breathtaking pans, tilts, and dolly shots without warping the fundamental geometry.
[Velocity Control] The subtle acceleration and deceleration in this panning sequence highlight the underlying physics-aware temporal modeling. Instead of a rigid linear shift, the camera motion feels organic and handheld, deeply enhancing the immersive storytelling experience.
[Parallax Effect Simulation] Notice the differing speeds of the foreground elements relative to the distant background. Generating a mathematically accurate multi-plane parallax from a single starting condition proves that our representations are not just 2D stretches, but robust 3D mappings.
[Aggressive Dolly Zoom] This is the classic "Vertigo effect" synthesized purely via generative constraints. The model concurrently manages a field of view (FOV) alteration while translating the camera position backwards. Keeping the primary subject precisely locked in scale demonstrates unparalleled spatial reasoning.
Special Camera Movement
[Module Overview] Traversing through complex spatial occlusion involves synthesizing parts of the environment that are initially invisible. This section pushes the boundary of 3D geometry hallucination through orbiting and fly-through motions.
[Fly-Through Obstructions] The camera physically pierces through foreground elements. To achieve this without glitching, OmniDirector continually generates plausible, physically accurate geometry on-the-fly, essentially creating unseen architectural data as it moves forward.
[360° Object Orbiting] Executing a smooth arc shot around a central focal point. Traditional models often flatten or tear textures when exposing the lateral sides of a subject. Here, the temporal network seamlessly infers anatomical depth and ambient lighting from previously occluded angles.
[Extreme Low Angle Sweeps] Initiating from a macro perspective and sweeping upwards creates drastic perspective distortion. The model elegantly calculates the vanishing points dynamically, ensuring that the background skyscrapers and environment maintain a hyper-realistic scale as the virtual lens tilts.
Pipeline
An MLLM Director first interprets multimodal instructions into high-level semantics and tells a storyline. Guided by this global planning, the first stage generates a blueprint video. In the second stage, keyframes are extracted from the blueprint and used as first–last frame conditions for parallel sub-clip generation, refining local details and dynamics to synthesize long-duration videos.
